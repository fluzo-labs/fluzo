# Community Conduct

Treat contributors respectfully and focus technical feedback on the work.
Harassment, threats, discrimination and exposing another person's private
information are not acceptable. Maintainers may moderate content or restrict
participation proportionately to protect the community.

Do not publish private reports or identifying details in issues. Serious conduct
violations may be reported using
[GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines).
This policy applies to official Fluzo collaboration spaces.
